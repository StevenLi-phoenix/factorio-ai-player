Version: 0.8.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - Optional fog of war (runtime setting ai-player-fog-of-war, default off;
      scripts/vision.lua). The engine never charts for a character without a
      player, so queries could "see" ore the AI never approached. With the
      setting on, get_resource_patch, inspect_entity, analyze_factory and
      map_warnings only report entities in KNOWN chunks (explored by the AI or
      charted by the force), get_enemies only VISIBLE ones, and goto refuses a
      target that is unexplored and more than 64 tiles away. Exploration is
      recorded either way, and the area around spawn is known from the start.
      map_warnings charted_only now means "known chunks" and also works with
      the setting off. Remote interface: set_fog_of_war.

Version: 0.7.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - Skill build_plan: place up to 100 entities (item, position, direction,

Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - Trigger technologies for the player-less AI character (scripts/triggers.lua).
      Factorio 2.0 gates the first techs behind actions (craft N, mine, build)
      that the engine credits only from player activity; the AI character has no
      LuaPlayer, so hand-crafting never counted and "craft a lab"
      (automation-science-pack) could not complete, locking the whole tech tree.
      AITriggers keeps a hand-craft ledger (called from every begin_crafting
      site) and re-derives each trigger from real state: production statistics
      plus the ledger for craft-item, mining products for mine-entity, an
      existing entity for build-entity. Nothing is marked researched without the
      work having been done; triggers that cannot be met on Nauvis are left
      alone. The research skill now names the exact pending condition. Remote
      interface: trigger_status.

Version: 0.5.1
Date: 2026-10-30
  Changes:
    - BREAKING: gather, deconstruct and build_ghosts no longer teleport to their
      targets. They act only on targets within character reach
      (can_reach_entity, build_distance for ghosts); otherwise they start a walk toward the nearest target
      and return a "walking ... retry next turn" result. build_ghosts no longer
      revives ghosts out of reach (ghost revive ignores character distance), and
      steps aside by walking when the character stands on a ghost.
    - pickup only collects ground items within reach.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Changes:
    - BREAKING: move, goto and return_home no longer teleport. They start a
      walk (scripts/movement.lua) that the character completes over the
      following ticks; the outcome (arrived / blocked / timeout) is appended to
      the next turn's action results. goto keeps its name and parameters.
      Prompts that assumed instant arrival must check the result next turn.
  Features:
    - AIMovement: asynchronous straight-line walking driven from on_tick
      (also while autonomy is disabled). No pathfinding; a blocked walk fails
      after ~45 ticks without progress instead of waiting for the timeout.
